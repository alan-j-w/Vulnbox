Code of Ethics

As a security practitioner and student of the VulnBox platform, you agree to uphold the highest standards of professional and ethical conduct. Our mission is to build builders and defenders.

Principle 1: Do No Harm

The skills developed on this platform are powerful. You swear to never invoke these techniques offensively in the wild against unconsenting networks, production software, or private individuals. Your goal is the preservation of confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Principle 2: Authorization

You must always operate within the scope of documented authorization. In the real world, this means possessing a written Rules of Engagement (RoE). On VulnBox, this means confining your attacks exclusively to the targets specified within the lab parameters.

Principle 3: Community & Respect

Share knowledge responsibly. Do not harass, dox, or sabotage fellow learners. Assist those who are struggling, but respect the learning journey by providing hints instead of direct answers.

Principle 4: Responsible Disclosure

If you identify a novel vulnerability outside of a controlled lab environment (e.g., in the VulnBox core portal), you will report it privately to the maintainers and allow a reasonable timeframe for remediation before public disclosure.
